1. Infancy & Childhood
嬰兒與兒童期

Focus: psychosocial development, attachment and early relationships.

重點：社會心理發展、依附，以及早期照顧關係。

Erikson: Psychosocial Development
Erikson 社會心理發展

Psychosocial crisis: a developmental tension involving the person and their social environment. It does not necessarily mean a psychiatric emergency.

社會心理危機：個人與社會環境之間的發展張力， 不一定代表精神科危機。

Virtue: an adaptive capacity developed through working with a stage's psychosocial tension.

美德／基本能力：透過處理某階段的發展任務， 逐漸形成的適應能力。

Infancy — Trust vs mistrust: can caregivers be relied upon? Associated virtue: hope.

嬰兒期——信任對不信任：照顧者是否可靠？ 對應的基本能力是希望。

Toddlerhood — Autonomy vs shame and doubt: can the child try to act independently? Virtue: will.

幼兒期——自主對羞愧與懷疑：孩子能否嘗試自己做事？ 對應的基本能力是意志。

Early childhood — Initiative vs guilt: can the child initiate activities and plans? Virtue: purpose.

兒童早期——主動對內疚：孩子能否主動構思及參與活動？ 對應的基本能力是目的感。

School age — Industry vs inferiority: can the child learn skills and feel capable? Virtue: competence.

學齡期——勤奮對自卑：孩子能否透過學習建立能力感？ 對應的基本能力是勝任感。

Example: A toddler tries to put on shoes. A caregiver allows time and offers suitable help. This supports autonomy.

例子：幼兒嘗試穿鞋，照顧者給予時間及適當幫助， 有助發展自主感。

Attachment Theory
依附理論

Attachment: a relationship in which a child seeks safety and comfort from a caregiver, especially when distressed.

依附：孩子特別在不安時，向照顧者尋求安全及安慰的關係。

Secure base: the caregiver provides a reliable source of comfort from which the child can explore.

安全基地：照顧者提供可靠支持，讓孩子安心探索， 有需要時可回來尋求安慰。

Secure: the child may be upset by separation, seeks comfort on reunion and can return to exploration.

安全型：分離時可能不安；重聚時尋求安慰， 然後可繼續探索。

Avoidant: the child tends to avoid or minimise contact on reunion. Little outward distress does not prove that the child feels no distress.

逃避型：重聚時傾向避免或減少接觸。 外表平靜不代表內心一定沒有壓力。

Resistant / ambivalent: the child seeks proximity but may resist comfort and remain distressed.

抗拒／矛盾型：孩子想接近照顧者，卻可能抗拒安慰， 並持續感到不安。

Disorganized: behaviour may appear contradictory, frozen, dazed or disoriented.

混亂型：行為可能互相矛盾，或出現僵住、 發呆及迷失方向的情況。

Exam reminder: one brief observation is not enough to diagnose an attachment pattern or establish its cause.

考試提醒：不能單憑一次短暫觀察診斷依附類型， 也不能直接推斷原因。

Social Referencing
社會參照

A child looks to a trusted person's emotional reaction to help interpret an unfamiliar situation.

孩子參考可信任人士的情緒反應，協助理解陌生情境。

Example: A child sees a new toy, looks at a caregiver's face and approaches after seeing encouragement.

例子：孩子看到新玩具，先看照顧者的表情； 看到鼓勵後才走近。

2. Adolescence
青春期

Focus: identity, exploration, commitment and parenting styles.

重點：身分認同、探索、承諾及教養方式。

Erikson: Identity vs Role Confusion
身分認同對角色混淆

Adolescents work towards a coherent sense of self while considering values, relationships, belonging and future roles.

青少年需要思考價值觀、關係、歸屬及未來角色， 逐漸建立較一致的自我認同。

Marcia: Identity Formation
Marcia 身分認同形成

Exploration: actively considering meaningful alternatives.

探索：主動考慮及比較有意義的不同選項。

Commitment: making a personally invested decision.

承諾：作出並投入一個個人選擇。

Diffusion: little or no exploration and little or no commitment.

迷失型：沒有或很少探索，也沒有明確承諾。

Foreclosure: commitment is present without meaningful exploration.

早閉型：已作決定，但之前沒有充分探索其他選項。

Moratorium: active exploration is occurring, but commitment is absent or not yet firm.

未定型：正在積極探索，但尚未有明確承諾。

Achievement: exploration has occurred and a personal commitment has been made.

定向型：經過探索後，作出個人承諾。

Foreclosure vs moratorium: foreclosure means committed without exploring; moratorium means exploring without a firm commitment.

早閉型與未定型：早閉型是「已決定、未探索」； 未定型是「正在探索、未決定」。

Example: “My family chose my career; I never considered alternatives” suggests foreclosure. “I am comparing careers and have not decided” suggests moratorium.

例子：「家人替我選職業，我沒有考慮其他選項」 偏向早閉型；「我正比較職業，尚未決定」偏向未定型。

A person's status can differ across domains, such as career and beliefs.

同一人在不同領域，例如職業及信仰，可以處於不同狀態。

Baumrind: Parenting Styles
Baumrind 教養方式

Demandingness: rules, expectations and guidance. Responsiveness: warmth and attention to the child's views.

要求：規則、期望及指導。 回應：溫暖，以及對孩子意見的重視。

Authoritative: high demandingness and high responsiveness. Clear limits are combined with explanation and listening.

威信型：高要求、高回應；設立清楚界線， 同時解釋及聆聽。

Authoritarian: high demandingness and low responsiveness. Emphasis is placed on obedience and control.

專制型：高要求、低回應；較強調服從及控制。

Permissive: low demandingness and high responsiveness. The caregiver is warm but sets few limits.

放任型：低要求、高回應；照顧者溫暖， 但較少設立界線。

Neglectful: low demandingness and low responsiveness. There is little guidance and emotional involvement.

疏忽型：低要求、低回應；缺少指導及情感參與。

Authoritative vs authoritarian: both can have rules. Look for warmth, explanation and responsiveness.

威信型與專制型：兩者都可能有規則； 要看是否有溫暖、解釋及聆聽。

Self-Concept & Self-Esteem
自我概念與自尊

Self-concept: how a person describes or understands themselves. Self-esteem: how they evaluate their worth.

自我概念：「我如何描述自己」。 自尊：「我如何評價自己的價值」。

This is an additional adolescence topic; check its exam scope.

這是青春期的延伸內容，請核對考試範圍。

3. Adulthood
成年期

Focus: intimacy, generativity, love and moral reasoning.

重點：親密關係、繁衍感、愛情及道德推理。

Erikson: Adult Tasks
Erikson 成人期任務

Intimacy vs isolation: developing close relationships while maintaining a sense of self.

親密對孤立：建立親密關係，同時維持自我感。

Generativity vs stagnation: caring for, guiding or contributing to others and future generations.

繁衍感對停滯：關心、培育或貢獻他人及下一代。 不只限於生育。

Sternberg: Triangular Theory of Love
Sternberg 愛情三角理論

Intimacy: closeness, warmth, connection and trust.

親密：接近感、溫暖、連結及信任。

Passion: romantic or physical attraction and desire.

激情：浪漫或身體上的吸引及渴望。

Commitment: a decision to love and maintain the relationship.

承諾：決定去愛，並維持這段關係。

Seven types of love／七種愛情類型

Liking: intimacy only.

喜歡：只有親密。

Infatuation: passion only.

迷戀：只有激情。

Empty love: commitment only.

空洞的愛：只有承諾。

Romantic love: intimacy + passion.

浪漫的愛：親密＋激情。

Companionate love: intimacy + commitment.

伴侶式的愛：親密＋承諾。

Fatuous love: passion + commitment.

愚昧式的愛：激情＋承諾，但親密較少。

Consummate love: intimacy + passion + commitment.

圓滿的愛：親密＋激情＋承諾。

Exam strategy: identify which components are described before naming the type.

答題方法：先找出題目描述了哪些成分， 再選擇愛情類型。

Kohlberg: Moral Reasoning
Kohlberg 道德推理

Focus on why a person makes a judgement, not only whether the answer is “yes” or “no”.

重點是當事人為甚麼這樣判斷， 而不只是答案「對」或「錯」。

Stage 1 — Punishment: obey to avoid punishment.

第 1 階段——懲罰：為避免懲罰而服從。

Stage 2 — Exchange: focus on personal needs and reciprocal benefit.

第 2 階段——交換：重視個人需要及互惠利益。

Stage 3 — Relationships: meet interpersonal expectations and be seen as a good person.

第 3 階段——人際關係：符合他人期望， 做一個「好人」。

Stage 4 — Social order: follow rules and duties to maintain order.

第 4 階段——社會秩序：遵守規則及履行責任， 以維持秩序。

Stage 5 — Social contract: protect rights and recognise that laws can be revised through social agreement.

第 5 階段——社會契約：重視權利， 並認為法律可透過社會協議修訂。

Stage 6 — Universal principles: follow internalised ethical principles, even when they conflict with rules.

第 6 階段——普遍原則：依循內化的倫理原則， 即使與現有規則衝突。

Stage 5 vs stage 6: stage 5 emphasises rights, agreement and revisable laws; stage 6 emphasises internalised universal principles.

第 5 與第 6 階段：第 5 階段重社會契約、 權利及可修訂的法律；第 6 階段重內化的普遍倫理原則。

4. Late Adulthood
晚年

Focus: life review, adjustment, daily functioning and SOC strategies.

重點：人生回顧、適應、日常功能及 SOC 策略。

Erikson: Integrity vs Despair
自我圓滿對絕望

Ego integrity: finding coherence and acceptance in one's life, including imperfect experiences.

自我圓滿：能整合及接受自己的一生， 包括不完美的經歷。

Despair: profound regret or a sense that life cannot be made meaningful. The associated virtue is wisdom.

絕望：對人生感到深重遺憾，或認為人生 無法找到意義。成功處理這個階段的基本能力是智慧。

Life review: invite a client to reflect on meaningful events and regrets without forcing a positive conclusion.

人生回顧：邀請當事人思考重要經歷及遺憾， 不強迫對方得出正面結論。

SOC: Selective Optimization with Compensation
選擇－優化－補償

SOC describes strategies for pursuing meaningful goals when resources are limited or changing. It can be used across the lifespan.

SOC 描述資源有限或改變時，如何追求有意義的目標； 並非只有長者才會使用。

Elective selection: choose a goal based on preference, without a loss prompting the choice.

自主選擇：按個人偏好選定目標， 並非因資源流失而作出改變。

Loss-based selection: change or reprioritise goals after a relevant resource is lost.

因流失而選擇：因能力或資源下降， 修改目標或重新排列優先次序。

Optimization: invest time, effort or training to improve pursuit of a goal.

優化：投入時間、努力或訓練， 提升達成目標的能力。

Compensation: use an alternative resource or method after loss to maintain a goal.

補償：因能力流失而使用替代資源或方法， 以維持原有目標。

Example: Reducing the number of activities after fatigue is loss-based selection. Using a magnifier after vision loss to keep reading is compensation.

例子：因疲倦而減少活動，是因流失而選擇； 因視力下降而用放大鏡繼續閱讀，是補償。

Key distinction: change the goal or its priority versus maintain the goal using another means.

關鍵分別：改變目標／次序，還是維持目標、 改用另一方法。

Peck: Additional Concepts
Peck 的補充概念

Ego differentiation: find self-worth beyond a former work role.

自我分化：在過往工作角色以外尋找自我價值。

Body transcendence: find enjoyment and meaning beyond physical limitations while attending to health needs.

超越身體：在身體限制以外尋找樂趣及意義， 同時仍照顧健康需要。

Ego transcendence: face mortality constructively and find meaning beyond one's individual lifespan.

超越自我：以建設性方式面對死亡， 並在個人生命以外尋找意義。

These are additional Lecture 9 concepts; check their exam scope.

這些是 Lecture 9 的補充概念，請核對考試範圍。

Age & Functioning
年齡與生活功能

Chronological age: age in years. Functional age: how a person performs daily roles.

實際年齡：按出生日期計算的歲數。 功能年齡：日常角色及活動的實際表現。

Biological age: the condition of body systems. Psychological age: how a person acts and feels.

生理年齡：身體系統的狀態。 心理年齡：一個人的行為及感受。

ADLs: basic personal care, such as eating, dressing, bathing and toileting.

ADL 日常生活功能：基本個人照顧， 例如進食、穿衣、洗澡及如廁。

IADLs: activities supporting independent living, such as shopping, managing money and medication.

IADL 工具性日常生活功能：支持獨立生活的活動， 例如購物、理財及管理藥物。

5. Social Identity, Power & Privilege
社會身分、權力與特權

Focus: how social positions relate to power, access and inequality.

重點：社會位置如何與權力、資源和不平等相連。

Key Distinctions
重要概念分別

Social identity: a social position or group membership, such as age, gender, ethnicity or disability status.

社會身分：個人的社會位置或群體身分， 例如年齡、性別、族裔及殘疾狀況。

Stereotype: a generalised belief about a group.

刻板印象：對某群體作概括性的看法。

Prejudice: an attitude or judgement based on group membership.

偏見：因一個人的群體身分而產生的態度或判斷。

Discrimination: unfair treatment related to social identity or group membership.

歧視：因社會身分或群體身分而受到的不公平待遇。

Oppression: a broader pattern of disadvantage maintained through power, institutions and social practices.

壓迫：由權力、制度及社會實踐維持的 較廣泛不利處境。

Example: assuming all older applicants cannot learn software is a stereotype. Refusing a qualified applicant an interview because of age is discrimination.

例子：認為所有年長求職者都學不懂軟件是刻板印象； 因年齡拒絕合資格者面試是歧視。

Privilege & the Coin Model
特權與硬幣模型

Privilege: an advantage associated with a social position in a particular context.

特權：在特定情境下，與某個社會位置相關的優勢。

Coin model: examine how advantage and oppression are connected within social systems, rather than considering only individual intentions.

硬幣模型：分析優勢與壓迫如何在同一社會制度中 互相連結，而不是只看個人意圖。

Example: a high-income wheelchair user may afford private transport but still face inaccessible buildings. Advantage and disadvantage can coexist.

例子：高收入輪椅使用者可能負擔得起私人交通， 但仍面對建築物無障礙不足；優勢與障礙可以同時存在。

Professional Reflexivity
專業反思

Social workers may influence assessments or access to services. Reflexivity means considering how one's role and assumptions affect the professional relationship.

社工可能影響評估及服務資源。反思是指留意自己的角色及假設， 如何影響與服務使用者的關係。

An unsuccessful application alone neither proves nor rules out discrimination. Explore the criteria, process and person's account.

單憑申請失敗不能證明或排除歧視；要了解標準、 過程及當事人的經驗。

6. Ecological Theory & Intersectionality
生態理論與交織性

Focus: the person in context and the interaction of social positions.

重點：把個人放在環境中理解，以及不同社會位置如何互相作用。

Bronfenbrenner's Ecological Systems
Bronfenbrenner 生態系統

Person: the active, developing individual.

個人：主動且持續發展的當事人。

Microsystem: a direct setting and the interactions within it, such as a child's classroom.

微系統：當事人直接參與的環境及互動， 例如孩子的課室。

Mesosystem: links between direct settings, such as a parent and teacher coordinating support.

中介系統：不同直接環境之間的連結， 例如家長與老師協調支援。

Exosystem: an indirect setting that affects the person, such as a parent's workplace affecting family routines.

外系統：當事人沒有直接參與、但會影響他們的環境， 例如父母工作場所改變家庭作息。

Macrosystem: broad cultural and institutional patterns, such as social beliefs and wider policy contexts.

宏系統：廣泛文化及制度模式， 例如社會信念及整體政策背景。

Chronosystem: time, transitions and historical changes affecting development.

時間系統：時間、生命轉變及歷史變化 對發展的影響。

Focal person: a workplace can be a microsystem for the parent who works there, but an exosystem for a child who is indirectly affected.

分析對象很重要：工作場所對直接工作的家長 可以是微系統；對間接受影響的孩子可以是外系統。

Lecture 10: Micro–Mezzo–Macro
Lecture 10 三層框架

Micro: individual biological, psychological, social and cognitive characteristics.

Micro 個人層面：個人的生理、心理、 社會及認知特徵。

Mezzo: interpersonal environments, such as family, friends, school and workplace relationships.

Mezzo 人際層面：家庭、朋友、學校及 工作場所等人際環境。

Macro: structural environments, such as policy, regulation and law.

Macro 結構層面：政策、規例及法律等結構環境。

Two frameworks, different labels: a child's direct interaction with a teacher is a Bronfenbrenner microsystem interaction. In Lecture 10's three-level approach, the interpersonal environment is mezzo.

兩套框架，用字不同：孩子與老師直接互動， 在 Bronfenbrenner 模型是 microsystem； 在 Lecture 10 三層框架，人際環境屬 mezzo。

Intersectionality
交織性

Multiple social positions interact with interpersonal and structural systems to shape experience. Intersectionality is more than simply adding identity labels together.

多重社會位置與人際及結構系統互相作用，共同塑造經驗。 交織性不只是把身分標籤相加。

Example: explore how gender, disability and income interact with hiring practices and transport access.

例子：探索性別、殘疾及收入， 如何與招聘制度及交通資源互相影響。

Single-axis analysis: examining one dimension at a time may miss experiences at the intersection of positions.

單軸分析：每次只看一個身分維度， 可能忽略多重社會位置交界處的經驗。

Iceberg Thinking
冰山思維

A visible behaviour is a starting point. Explore current and past individual, relational and structural influences without assuming a cause before assessment.

可見行為只是起點。應探索現在及過去的個人、人際和結構因素， 但不能未經評估就假設原因。

7. Stress Response
壓力反應

Focus: stressors, responses, bodily adaptation and ACE scoring.

重點：壓力源、壓力反應、身體調節及 ACE 計分。

Stressor, Stress & Response
壓力源、壓力與反應

Stressor: an external event or circumstance that challenges adaptation.

壓力源：需要個人適應的外在事件或處境。

Stress: a subjective state associated with appraising demands as exceeding available resources.

壓力：個人認為外在要求超出可用應對資源時， 所產生的主觀狀態。

Stress response: physiological and behavioural responses that help the body deal with a challenge.

壓力反應：身體為應對挑戰而出現的生理及行為反應。

Example: job loss is a stressor; worrying that savings cannot cover rent is part of the stress experience; increased heart rate can be a stress response.

例子：失業是壓力源；擔心積蓄不足以付租金 是壓力經驗；心跳加快可以是壓力反應。

Three Response Categories
三類壓力反應

Positive stress: a manageable challenge is met and can build a sense of mastery.

良性壓力：可應付的挑戰被處理， 並可能建立掌握感。

Tolerable stress: a serious negative event is buffered by sufficient personal or social support.

可容忍壓力：嚴重負面事件受到足夠的 個人或社會支持緩衝。

Toxic stress: prolonged or severe adversity, especially without adequate support, may lead to excessive stress-system activation.

惡性壓力：長期或嚴重逆境，尤其缺乏足夠支持時， 可能導致壓力系統過度啟動。

Consider severity, duration, frequency and support. One ACE does not automatically prove toxic stress.

要看嚴重程度、持續時間、頻率及支持。 一個 ACE 不自動等於惡性壓力。

Homeostasis & Allostasis
體內平衡與身體調適

Homeostasis: maintaining physiological balance within a workable range.

體內平衡：把生理狀態維持在可運作的範圍。

Allostasis: adaptive changes that help the body meet demands and manage threats.

身體調適：身體透過改變來應付需求及威脅。

Allostatic load: cumulative physiological burden associated with repeated or prolonged adaptation.

身體調適負荷：反覆或長期調節所累積的生理負擔。

A temporary rise in heart rate during danger can be adaptive. Repeated or prolonged activation may contribute to cumulative burden.

面對危險時心跳暫時加快可以有保護作用； 反覆或長期啟動則可能增加累積負擔。

SNS, HPA & Hormones
神經系統、HPA 軸與荷爾蒙

SNS: the sympathetic nervous system contributes to rapid responses, including increased heart rate and breathing.

SNS 交感神經系統：參與快速反應， 包括心跳及呼吸加快。

Adrenaline: associated with rapid mobilisation during a stress response.

腎上腺素：與壓力反應中的快速動員有關。

HPA axis: hypothalamus → pituitary → adrenal glands.

HPA 軸：下丘腦 → 腦下垂體 → 腎上腺。

Cortisol: participates in stress adaptation and helps increase glucose availability.

皮質醇：參與壓力調適， 並有助增加葡萄糖的可用量。

Amygdala: involved in threat-related emotion. Hippocampus: important for memory.

杏仁核：參與與威脅相關的情緒處理。 海馬體：對記憶很重要。

ACEs: Ten-Category Framework
ACE 傳統十類框架

ACEs are a subset of childhood adversity. In this conventional home-based framework, each supported category counts once.

ACE 是童年逆境的一部分。在傳統家庭相關框架中， 每個有資料支持的類別計一次。

Abuse: physical abuse, emotional abuse and sexual abuse.

虐待：身體虐待、情緒虐待及性虐待。

Neglect: physical neglect and emotional neglect.

忽視：身體忽視及情緒忽視。

Household challenges: parental separation or divorce; household substance misuse; household mental illness; violence against the mother; an incarcerated household member.

家庭挑戰：父母分居或離婚、家庭成員物質濫用、 家庭成員精神疾病、對母親施暴，以及家庭成員入獄。

Scoring: count categories, not incidents. Count only what the case supports. If other categories have not been assessed, the complete score may be unknown.

計分：按類別而不是事件次數計算。 只計題目有資料支持的類別；其他類別未評估時， 完整分數可能仍未知。

Example: emotional abuse + physical neglect + household substance misuse = 3 categories.

例子：情緒虐待＋身體忽視＋家庭成員物質濫用 ＝ 3 個類別。

Broader adversity: bullying, community violence and discrimination can matter even when they are not counted in this conventional ten-category score.

更廣泛的童年逆境：欺凌、社區暴力及歧視 即使不計入這個傳統十類分數，仍然值得重視。

An ACE score is not a diagnosis and does not determine an individual's future.

ACE 分數不是診斷，也不會決定個人的未來。
